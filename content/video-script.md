# DentalCraft - Presentation video script (~75 s)

Format: 16:9, no voice-over required (text-led). Optional VO provided at the end.
Total runtime: 75 s. On-screen text in English; Italian subtitle line under each.
Rule: every animation is a concept illustration of the intended workflow, not a demo of working features. Scenes 3-6 carry the corner label "Concept - intended workflow" / "Concetto - flusso previsto". Scene 7 shows real screens, labelled "Work in progress".
Music: calm, precise, minimal electronic/piano with soft pulse; builds gradually, resolves at the end card.

### Scene 1 - Brand reveal (0:00-0:06)
- **Visual:** Dark neutral background. A thin line draws itself into the curve of a dental arch, then resolves into the DentalCraft mark. Wordmark fades in.
- **On-screen (EN):** DentalCraft
- **Subtitle (IT):** DentalCraft
- **Music:** Single soft tone, a quiet pulse begins at 0:03.

### Scene 2 - What it is (0:06-0:14)
- **Visual:** Mark shrinks to the corner. A Blender-style viewport sketch dissolves into a clean, dedicated application window.
- **On-screen (EN):** A dental CAD, built native. / From a Blender add-on to a standalone app.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Un CAD odontoiatrico, costruito nativo. / Da add-on di Blender ad applicazione autonoma.
- **Music:** Pulse steady, light plucked notes added.

### Scene 3 - Scan to arch (0:14-0:24)
- **Visual:** *(Concept)* A raw intraoral scan mesh rotates into view. File chips slide in: STL, OBJ, PLY. The mesh orients onto an arch curve; FDI numbers appear above the teeth.
- **On-screen (EN):** Start from the scan. / STL, OBJ, PLY. Arch. FDI numbering.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Si parte dalla scansione. / STL, OBJ, PLY. Arcata. Numerazione FDI.
- **Music:** Rhythm becomes more defined.

### Scene 4 - Segmentation (0:24-0:34)
- **Visual:** *(Concept)* Teeth separate by colour, one by one, from the gingiva. Light sweep across the arch.
- **On-screen (EN):** Segment. Reconstruct. Diagnose.
- **Sub-line (EN):** Space analysis, Little index, Bolton.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Segmenta. Ricostruisci. Diagnostica. / Analisi dello spazio, indice di Little, Bolton.
- **Music:** Adds a low sustained layer.

### Scene 5 - Staging (0:34-0:47)
- **Visual:** *(Concept)* The final position appears as a translucent ghost arch. Teeth glide through a timeline of stages 1, 2, 3 ... N, each with a small limit marker. IPR and attachment icons flicker on individual teeth.
- **On-screen (EN):** Plan the final position. / Stage by stage, within per-tooth limits.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Pianifica la posizione finale. / Stage dopo stage, entro i limiti di ogni dente.
- **Music:** Builds; subtle rising arpeggio.

### Scene 6 - Aligner shell (0:47-0:55)
- **Visual:** *(Concept)* A transparent shell wraps the arch. Trim line traces along the gingival edge; a small identification stamp appears; a print-check tick sweeps across. Export chip: STL per stage.
- **On-screen (EN):** Shell, trim line, stamp. / Export STL per stage.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Shell, linea di taglio, marcatura. / Esportazione STL per ogni stage.
- **Music:** Near peak, bright but restrained.

### Scene 7 - The app today (0:55-1:03)
- **Visual:** Real screen captures: Home, then the Aligners workspace with the 3D viewport. Soft zoom and parallax. Badge top-right: "Work in progress".
- **On-screen (EN):** The application today: shell, project format, 3D viewport, scan import. / Clinical algorithms: being ported and validated.
- **Subtitle (IT):** L'applicazione oggi: struttura, formato di progetto, viewport 3D, importazione scansioni. / Algoritmi clinici: in porting e validazione.
- **Music:** Holds steady; slight filter opening.

### Scene 8 - Modules roadmap (1:03-1:10)
- **Visual:** Five tabs slide in: Aligners (highlighted, "in development"), Model Builder, Splint, Crown & Bridge, Implant (greyed, "planned"). No dates.
- **On-screen (EN):** Aligners first. More modules planned.
- **Subtitle (IT):** Prima gli Allineatori. Altri moduli in programma.
- **Music:** Peak, then begins to resolve.

### Scene 9 - End card (1:10-1:15)
- **Visual:** Clean background, mark centred. Badge "In development". URL below. Disclaimer in a clear, legible bar (not fine print).
- **On-screen (EN):** In development. / dentalcraft.app / In development. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use.
- **Subtitle (IT):** In sviluppo. / dentalcraft.app / In sviluppo. Non è un dispositivo medico. Non per uso clinico.
- **Music:** Resolves on a single sustained chord; short tail.
